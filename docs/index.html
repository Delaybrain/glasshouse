Glasshouse

A fast, glassy home screen for Android TV and Google TV. Real screenshots from a 1.9 GB TV.
Get it on GitHub

Click the highlighted spots, anywhere else for the next screen, or use the arrow keys.
Glasshouse grew out of tv-detox by Ansub Khan and started with android-tv-debloat-toolkit by seun-novodev, both MIT. App names and logos belong to their owners.
Use at your own risk: changing a TV's system apps can leave it needing a factory reset. No warranty, no liability (MIT).
